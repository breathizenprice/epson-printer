<h2>Epson EcoTank L3110 Specifications</h2>

<h3>Printing Technology</h3>
<p>The device uses Epson's Micro Piezo inkjet printing technology to produce documents and images.</p>

<h3>Print Resolution</h3>
<p>The printer supports a maximum print resolution of up to 5760 × 1440 optimized dpi. Actual output quality depends on the selected settings, paper type, ink, and original document.</p>

<h3>Print Speed</h3>
<p>Epson lists ISO print speeds of approximately 10 pages per minute for black documents and 5 pages per minute for color documents. Actual performance varies according to printing conditions.</p>

<h3>Scanner Resolution</h3>
<p>The integrated flatbed scanner provides an optical resolution of 600 × 1200 dpi.</p>

<h3>Connectivity</h3>
<p>The standard L3110 uses a USB connection and does not include built-in Wi-Fi or Ethernet networking.</p>

<h3>Ink System</h3>
<p>The printer uses four ink colors: black, cyan, magenta, and yellow. Compatible ink bottle codes may vary by region.</p>

<h2>Epson L3110 Red Lights Blinking: What Does It Mean?</h2>

<p>The printer uses indicator lights to communicate operating conditions. Different blinking patterns can represent different problems.</p>

<h3>Paper Jam Warning</h3>
<p>Paper may be trapped inside the feeding mechanism. Follow the documented procedure for removing jammed paper.</p>

<h3>Paper Feed Problem</h3>
<p>Incorrectly loaded paper may prevent normal operation. Reload compatible paper and adjust the guides.</p>

<h3>Waste Ink Pad Service Warning</h3>
<p>Inkjet printers generate waste ink during maintenance operations. Internal absorbent materials collect this ink, and some printers use maintenance counters to estimate when servicing is required.</p>

<h3>Internal Printer Error</h3>
<p>A mechanical or electronic problem may prevent the device from operating. Check the exact warning pattern using the official user guide.</p>

<h2>Understanding Epson L3110 Waste Ink Pad Errors</h2>

<h3>Why Does the Printer Display a Service Warning?</h3>
<p>The printer may estimate that its waste ink collection system has reached a maintenance threshold.</p>

<h3>Can a Software Reset Fix the Problem?</h3>

<h3>Should You Use a Free Reset Utility?</h3>
<p>Be cautious when downloading adjustment programs from unknown websites. Unverified software may contain malware or incompatible maintenance commands.</p>

<h3>When Is Professional Service Necessary?</h3>

## When professional service is appropriate

Professional help may be appropriate when the printer has a persistent service warning, an interrupted firmware update, visible ink leakage, mechanical damage, or repeated errors after normal troubleshooting. Describe the symptoms precisely rather than requesting a generic reset. Ask what physical maintenance, parts, or labor are involved before authorizing work.

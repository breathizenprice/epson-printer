## Common problems and what to check

### Computer cannot find the printer

Check the USB cable and try a different USB port; avoid hubs during initial setup.

### Printing works but scanning fails

Install the compatible scanner component and check operating system permissions.

### Installation fails

Remove conflicting incomplete installations and retry using the manufacturer’s instructions.

## When professional service is appropriate

Professional help may be appropriate when the printer has a persistent service warning, an interrupted firmware update, visible ink leakage, mechanical damage, or repeated errors after normal troubleshooting. Describe the symptoms precisely rather than requesting a generic reset. Ask what physical maintenance, parts, or labor are involved before authorizing work.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does L3110 have Wi-Fi?

The standard L3110 does not include built-in Wi-Fi; it uses USB.

### Do I need a separate scanner driver?

### Can I install without a CD?

### Why does the printer appear offline?
